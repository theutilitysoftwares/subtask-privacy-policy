Privacy Policy

Subtask · Last updated: October 1, 2026

Subtask does not collect, share, or sell your personal data. Everything you write stays on your device.

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the Subtask app ("the app", "we") handles your information. The app is developed by Jackson, an independent developer.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The tasks and subtasks you create are stored only in a local database on your device. They are never sent to us or to any server.

Permissions

The app does not request any special permissions, such as location, contacts, camera, microphone, or storage.

Internet and third parties

  • The app works fully offline and does not need an internet connection.
  • The app does not contain ads, analytics, or tracking tools.
  • The app does not share data with any third party.

Data storage and deletion

Your data lives only on your device. You can delete individual tasks inside the app, or remove all data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
